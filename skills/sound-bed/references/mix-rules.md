# Mix rules

- Targets: -16 LUFS integrated for narrated explainers, -14 for reels and shorts; true peak -1.5 dBTP on the mix
  so the AAC encode stays under -1.0.
- Voice first: music ducks 12-16 dB under speech; the bed should be at least 18 LU under the voice at every
  sentence start. Between sections the bed can breathe back up.
- SFX levels: -8 to -12 dB relative to the mix for entrances; impacts can go to -6 on a lock-up; never louder than
  the voice. Lead the picture by 30-60 ms: sound that lands exactly with the frame reads as late.
- Density: one SFX per idea. A sound on every element is noise, and repeated identical whooshes read as a template.
- Pre-drop gap: duck the music to silence for half a beat before the biggest hit.
- Endings: let the last hit ring 1.5-2.5 s under the end card; fade the bed rather than cutting it.
- Measured references: other people's reels often master at -12 LUFS with -0.6 dBTP peaks; match the platform
  target, not the reference.
