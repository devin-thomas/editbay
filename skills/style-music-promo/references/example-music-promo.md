# Illustrative music-promo brief

This is a neutral planning example, not a report of a completed production.

Use an original instrumental track and a fictional application with a generic icon. Start the soundtrack at its first intended note; do not add extra sounds or alter the artist's track without approval. Tie cuts and typography to measured beats. Show a real local capture only after checking it for personal information.

Build from the templates in `../assets/kit/`, supply project-local assets, and record the chosen timing in the beat sheet. Prepare landscape and portrait layouts independently. Verify the encoded output, caption safety, and loudness; retain the measurements in the local project, not in this toolkit.

Before delivery, check the overview, every cut, the final frame, and the soundtrack. Mark unperformed checks as pending. Publishing the finished piece is a separate decision from producing it.
