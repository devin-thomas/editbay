# Pacing a rough cut

- `--keep-gap` is the breath left where dead air was removed. 0.25-0.35 s sounds natural for explainers; 0.15-0.2 s
  for fast shorts; 0.4-0.6 s for reflective or emotional delivery. A cut with no air sounds robotic.
- `--max-gap` decides what counts as dead air. Below 0.5 s you start cutting phrasing, not silence.
- Fillers: remove standalone "um/uh/erm". Leave "like" and "you know" unless the speaker asks: they carry rhythm
  and meaning, and removing them mid-phrase leaves jumps you can hear.
- Retakes: when a speaker restarts, the second take is the keeper almost always. When the first take has the better
  energy and only the ending failed, protect it and cut the second take by hand in the EDL.
- Every removal on camera is a potential jump cut. Two jumps within 3 s read as a glitch; group them under one punch-in
  or one piece of B-roll in the beat sheet.
- Protect laughs, reactions, and the pause before a punchline. The transcript cannot see them; the keep list and the
  strips can.
