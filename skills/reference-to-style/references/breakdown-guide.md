# Writing a breakdown

- **Timeline:** one row per chapter or scene. Time range from the cuts and holds in MEASUREMENTS.md, a neutral name
  for what the chapter does ("kinetic type", "material study"), what is on screen described in your own words, and
  the hand-off (a transformation, a flash, a hard cut, a hold).
- **Principles:** 6-10 numbered statements of cause, each with evidence: "Cuts sit on the grid: 7 of 12 cut events
  within 2 frames of a half-beat after removing the detector lag." Prefer mechanisms (one motif carries the piece;
  density breathes; hand-offs are transformations) over adjectives (clean, premium, dynamic).
- **Signature:** the specific choices that identify the source: exact layouts and labels, the text it says, the order
  of its chapters, a memorable final gesture. The style keeps the principles and sources its own signature from the
  brand and the subject.
- **Quotes:** keep any on-screen text you cite short; describe rather than transcribe.
- Worked example: examples/style-from-kenn-reel/reference/BREAKDOWN.md in the editbay repository.
