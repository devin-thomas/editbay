# The five-step edit loop (explainer example)

> Spoken layer only. Tone: first person, plain, show then explain; no "in this video", no call to action.
> Disclosure (on screen at the end, not spoken): Narrated with a voice model trained on the creator's own voice.

[B01] You can hand a video to a coding agent and get a real edit back. What makes it work is a loop, not a lucky prompt.

[B02] Step one is transcribe. Every word gets a timestamp, so every graphic can land on the word it belongs to.

[B03] Step two is cut. Dead air, filler, and retakes come out, and every cut lands in silence.

[B04] Step three is plan the beats. Each graphic gets a word to land on, a place on screen, and a reason to exist.

[B05] Step four is build. The agent writes the motion as a web page and renders it frame by frame.

[B06] Step five is verify. It measures the loudness, checks every cut and every card, and fixes what fails before you watch.

[B07] Then the loop feeds itself. What you liked and what you didn't goes back into the skills, so the next edit starts smarter.
